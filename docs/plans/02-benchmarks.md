# 02 Benchmarks

Parent: [Master plan](README.md)

Status: `not-started`

Depends on [01 Workforce](01-workforce.md) being `done`.

## Outcome

A taxonomy version and a benchmark can be published against a job profile. The published version cannot be edited. A change is a new version. History is queryable. Drafts are not visible to employees. Ladders and levels come from data, not from a fixed list in code.

## Out of scope

Assessment sittings, learning paths, packs, and seals. Publication here is the immutable version. The competency-lead seal is slice 05.
