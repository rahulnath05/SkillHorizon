# 15 Assessment bank

Parent: [Master plan](README.md)

Status: `not-started`

Depends on [14 Activation](14-activation.md) being `done`.

## Outcome

The assessment studio opens the item bank, rubric and marking, and the SME panel. The next item in a sitting is chosen from the previous answer. Item text, rubrics, and response bodies stay in the assessment database. The sealed result still stores proficiencies and a result id. An employee grant does not open the bank.

## Out of scope

Mira’s retrieval corpus, response text in the domain database, and the operations reports in slice 16.
