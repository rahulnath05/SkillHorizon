# 10 Manager workspace

Parent: [Master plan](README.md)

Status: `done`

Depends on [09 Employee tasks](09-employee-tasks.md) being `done`.

## Outcome

The people-manager workspace opens Overview, My Team, Reviews & Evidence, Coaching, and Team Skills. Every row is a direct report under the management relationship in force. Overview and team position come from a snapshot with an as-of time. Evidence validation uses the validation command already built. A profile correction can be filed. The queue that works that request is slice 11.

## Out of scope

Practice heatmaps, the item bank, response text, and Mira.

## Micro-steps

| Step | Work | Status |
