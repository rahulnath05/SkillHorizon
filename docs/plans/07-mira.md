# 07 Mira

Parent: [Master plan](README.md)

Status: `not-started`

Depends on [17 Platform admin](17-platform-admin.md) being `done`. It is not the next slice. Slices 08 through 17 come first.

## Outcome

A Python service behind the learning-service port conducts the employee conversation, including the assessment sitting. The learning service records the sealed result. Mira cannot publish a benchmark, seal a practice, or approve evidence. She has no write role on those tables. If aggregate export has an agreed contract, the worker can push counts and distributions. Item text stays out of her retrieval corpus.

## Out of scope

Unsupervised writes to benchmarks, seals, grants, or validations. Item-bank content in the retrieval corpus. A change of language for the core API.
